About Me

Emily is a Chinese Cambodian American currently studying at San Jose State University. Emily is a digital media artist based in San Diego, CA. With just a pencil and paper, there are endless possibilities in creating art. By starting with traditional art and gradually transitioning to digital art in her artistic journey, She aim to create art that reflects society and nature.
